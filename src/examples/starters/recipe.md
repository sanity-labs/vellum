# Weeknight Mushroom Ragù

A rich, meaty-tasting pasta sauce with no meat in it. Most of the time is hands-off simmering.

Serves: 4
Prep: 15 minutes
Cook: 40 minutes
Difficulty: Easy
Cuisine: Italian

## Ingredients

- 500 g mixed mushrooms, finely chopped
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 can (400 g) crushed tomatoes
- 150 ml red wine
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- Olive oil, salt and pepper
- 400 g pappardelle, to serve

## Method

1. Heat a good glug of olive oil in a wide pan. Fry the mushrooms over high heat until they stop giving off water and start to brown, about 10 minutes.
2. Add the onion and cook until soft. Stir in the garlic and tomato paste for one minute.
3. Pour in the wine and let it bubble away by half.
4. Add the tomatoes and thyme. Simmer, partly covered, for 25 minutes. Season.
5. Toss with the cooked pasta and a splash of the pasta water.
