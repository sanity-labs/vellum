# Senior Frontend Engineer, Studio

Team: Studio Experience
Location: Oslo or London
Workplace: Hybrid
Employment type: Full-time
Apply: https://example.com/careers/senior-frontend-studio

The salary band is being finalized with People Ops. Do not publish a number yet.

You'll work on the editing experience thousands of content teams open every morning. The job is making complex content feel simple to edit, and making it fast.

## What you'll do

- Build and ship features in the Studio, from the first sketch to the release notes
- Work with design on interaction details that most people never notice until they're wrong
- Profile and fix performance problems in a large React codebase
- Review code and help other engineers grow

## What we're looking for

- Five or more years building production web apps with React and TypeScript
- You care about accessibility and can explain why a pattern fails a screen reader
- You've owned something end to end and lived with the consequences
- Experience with real-time collaboration or CRDTs is a plus
