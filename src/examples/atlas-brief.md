Frequently asked questions

Can we start with one team?
Yes. Start with a single workflow and invite more teams when you are ready. Your first workspace does not need to model the entire organization.

Does Atlas replace our existing tools?
No. Keep the tools your team already uses. Atlas is the shared context around decisions, ownership, and handoffs.

Can we export our work?
Yes. Project plans and checklists can be exported as JSON. Your team keeps a portable copy of its content.
